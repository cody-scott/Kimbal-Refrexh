## Suggested paths

- **Four-week core:** Week 1: Ch. 1–3 + Modules 0–2. Week 2: Ch. 4–5 + Modules 1 and 3. Week 3: Ch. 6–7 + Module 4.
  Week 4: Ch. 8–9 + Modules 5–6 and the capstone.
- **Interview prep in a weekend:** Ch. 2 + the [cheat sheet](/reference/cheat-sheet) + [interview questions](/practice/interview-questions)
  + [spot the error](/practice/spot-the-error).

## Other Kimball sources

- **Kimball Group website**: the [techniques list](https://www.kimballgroup.com/data-warehouse-business-intelligence-resources/kimball-techniques/dimensional-modeling-techniques/)
  and the archive of **Design Tips** and articles.
- ***The Kimball Group Reader*** (2nd ed., 2016): the collected articles and design tips, organized by topic.
- ***The Data Warehouse Lifecycle Toolkit*** (2nd ed., 2008): the full project lifecycle, from requirements to deployment.
