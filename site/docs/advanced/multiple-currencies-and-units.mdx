# Multiple currencies and units of measure

<KimballRef chapters={[6, 7]} techniques={['Multiple Currency Facts', 'Multiple Units of Measure Facts']} />

## Multiple currencies

Cascade's web store sells in USD, CAD and EUR. Analysts want:

- **local** amounts (what the Canadian customer actually paid), for regional teams and reconciliation;
- **standard** amounts in one reporting currency (USD), for anything that sums across currencies.

Kimball's recommendation: store **both** on the fact row, plus a **currency dimension** identifying the local
currency.

<DbtFile path="models/marts/orders/fct_order_lines.sql" lines={[27, 52]} />

Design points:

- **Convert at the transaction-date rate** and store the result, so history doesn't shift when rates change.
- Never sum the `*_local` columns across currencies: filter or group by `local_currency_key` first.
- If finance needs other conversion bases (month-end rate, budget rate), add a **separate fact column per
  basis**, or a currency-conversion fact table keyed by date and currency pair for on-the-fly conversion into
  any currency.

```sql
-- Revenue by local currency, and the USD equivalent
select c.currency_code,
       sum(f.net_order_amount_local) as net_local,
       sum(f.net_order_amount_usd)   as net_usd
from fct_order_lines f
join dim_currency c on c.currency_key = f.local_currency_key
group by 1;
```

## Multiple units of measure

A product might be ordered in cases, shipped in pallets and sold in eaches. Different teams want quantities in
different units.

Kimball's guidance: store quantities in **one standard unit** on the fact (or a small set of "conformed" units),
and put the **conversion factors** on the product dimension (or on the fact, if they vary by transaction):

```sql
-- fact stores quantity_each; dim_product stores each_per_case, each_per_pallet
select p.sku,
       sum(f.quantity_each)                    as eaches,
       sum(f.quantity_each) / p.each_per_case  as cases
from fct_shipments f
join dim_product p on p.product_key = f.product_key
group by p.sku, p.each_per_case;
```

Don't store five quantity columns in five units; that's five chances to be inconsistent.
