# Module 0: Foundations

Before memorizing techniques, internalize the **two goals** that every Kimball decision serves:

1. **Understandability.** A business person should look at the model and recognize their business.
2. **Query performance.** Queries should be simple, predictable joins that a database (or BI tool) can optimize.

Almost every rule you'll learn (denormalize dimensions, declare the grain, use surrogate keys, conform your
dimensions) is a consequence of those two goals.
